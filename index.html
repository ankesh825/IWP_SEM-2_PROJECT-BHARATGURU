Indore: The Cleanest City & Food Capital of India

Home to Devi Ahilya Vishwavidyalaya, Indore seamlessly blends grand Maratha-Holkar royal legacy with India’s most celebrated culinary scenes at Sarafa and Chappan Dukan.

🏰

Rajwada & Lal Bagh

7-storey Holkar dynasty palace architecture with French-inspired interiors and lush garden estates.

🍢

Sarafa Night Market

Jewelry market by day transforming into India’s famous midnight street food paradise (Garadu, Bhutte Ka Kees, Joshi Dahi Bada).

🌸

Chappan Dukan

Indore's iconic 56-shop gourmet walkway serving world-famous Poha-Jalebi, sev varieties, and artisanal sweets.

🕉

Mahakal Lok Corridor (Ujjain)

Just 55 km from Indore, experience the magnificent spiritual corridor of Lord Mahakaleshwar Jyotirlinga.
